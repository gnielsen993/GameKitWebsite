Accessibility

Use the GameDrawer website with a keyboard, a screen reader or your device's display settings.

Find your way around

Press Tab to reach "Skip to content" at the start of a page. A teal outline shows keyboard focus. When you open a full app screenshot, press Escape or choose Close to return to the link that opened it.

Try the Video Mode preview

In the Video Mode preview, use Tab to reach the board and the six video location buttons. On the board, the arrow keys move between squares. Press Space or Enter to use the selected action, Reveal or Flag. Press F to flag or unflag a square at any time. Each square tells screen readers its row, its column and what it shows.

The preview announces a win or a loss, then moves focus to Play again.

Display settings

The site follows your light or dark appearance. Reduced motion turns off smooth scrolling, the moving video bar and the layout animation in the preview.

Report a problem

Contact GameDrawer support with the page, device and what you were trying to do.
